Privacy Policy

Beyond Strategy Pass · Last updated 25 November 2026

Beyond Strategy Pass is the entry-pass website for Beyond Strategy: Consulting & Career Lab (12 December 2026), run by MOKRA Consulting, a student-led consulting club. This page explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your registration details, from the registration form you filled in: name, email, phone number, university, faculty and year.
  • Your Google account email, if you choose "Continue with Google". Google also shares your name and profile picture with the sign-in; we only use the email address.
  • Your check-in record on event day: the time you were checked in and which committee member scanned your pass.

Why we use it

  • To match you to the list of confirmed participants and show your personal entry QR code.
  • To check you in at registration and know who has arrived.
  • To contact you about this event if needed.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside the MOKRA Consulting event committee.

Where it's kept

The guest list is kept in a Google Sheet owned by the MOKRA Consulting Google account and is processed by Google Apps Script. Only the event committee can open it. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. To show the page, your browser also loads fonts from Google Fonts and a QR-code tool from jsDelivr. These services may see technical details such as your IP address, as with any website.

Your phone or computer remembers the email you signed in with and your "Pack your bag" ticks, so you don't have to sign in again. You can clear this any time with Sign out or by clearing your browser data.

How long we keep it

We keep the guest list and check-in records only as long as we need them to run and follow up on this event, and delete them within 3 months after 12 December 2026.

Your choices

You can always use your email instead of Google sign-in. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email mokraconsulting@gmail.com.
